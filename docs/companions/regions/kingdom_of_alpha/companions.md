# Companions — Kingdom of Alpha

## Companion Profile: Bridgitte

- **Companion Name:** Bridgitte
- **Companion Type:** combat
- **Race:** human female
- **Primary Role:** tank (evasion)
- **Secondary Role (optional):** DPS (sustained melee)
- **Region:** kingdom_of_alpha
- **Location:** arkama_village
- **Sublocation:** bridgitte_house
- **Region Status:** active_now
- **Location Status:** active_now
- **Sublocation Status:** active_now
- **First Meet Context:** After the chief’s handoff, the player visits Bridgitte at her house; she evaluates the player’s condition, lends an old dagger, and starts practical training through the nearby coyote culling task.
- **Identity Summary:** A measured former adventurer with noble roots and nerves of steel, now living quietly in Arkama Village while teaching survival-first combat discipline.
- **Questline Hook:** Bridgitte is a noble lady who did not inherit title and left home; later, her success sparked jealousy from her sister (the baroness), pressuring Bridgitte to leave adventuring, and her arc focuses on proving she is no threat.
- **Personality / Social Tags:** measured, ambitious, nerves of steel, noble
- **Distinctiveness Notes:** Bridgitte is road-forged and monster-tested from years of active adventuring, contrasting with Sonja’s sheltered church upbringing and prophecy-centered social arc.

## Companion Profile: Kaela

- **Companion Name:** Kaela
- **Companion Type:** combat
- **Race:** human female
- **Primary Role:** DPS
- **Secondary Role (optional):** support
- **Region:** kingdom_of_alpha
- **Location:** arkama_village
- **Sublocation:** arkama_tavern
- **Region Status:** active_now
- **Location Status:** active_now
- **Sublocation Status:** active_now
- **First Meet Context:** The player finds Kaela in the Arkama tavern after a rough hunt report; she challenges the player’s field judgment over drinks, then offers to run live tracking drills outside the village.
- **Identity Summary:** A road-worn beast-tracker who values practical results over heroics, Kaela fights with relentless pressure and teaches survival through observation, pacing, and controlled aggression.
- **Questline Hook:** Kaela is tracking an unusually coordinated predator trail near Arkama and wants the player as a second set of eyes; the questline escalates from routine culls into uncovering who or what is driving pack behavior.
- **Personality / Social Tags:** pragmatic, dry-witted, vigilant, disciplined, unsentimental
- **Distinctiveness Notes:** Unlike Bridgitte’s mentor-guardian tone and Sonja’s prophecy-faith arc, Kaela’s identity centers on active field hunting, pattern-reading, and a low-romance, high-competence partnership dynamic.

- **Companion Name:** Duchess Naya of House Asin
- **Companion Type:** combat
- **Race:** night elf
- **Primary Role:** support (focus healer)
- **Secondary Role (optional):** DPS (magic, fire mage)
- **Region:** kingdom_of_alpha
- **Location:** arkama_village
- **Sublocation:** arkama_tavern
- **Region Status:** active_now
- **Location Status:** active_now
- **Sublocation Status:** active_now
- **First Meet Context:** After the Coyote King is defeated, Naya—who already had an earlier encounter with the player—witnesses the player’s heroics in Arkama and offers a different class of work.
- **Identity Summary:** A mystical night-elf duchess with commanding presence, Naya blends strict standards with restorative magic, expecting excellence from those she trusts.
- **Questline Hook:** Despite her royal blood, Naya is seen as neglecting formal duties; her companion arc is about proving to the Night Elves of Tau that her chosen work has real value.
- **Personality / Social Tags:** noble, mystical, haughty, commanding
- **Distinctiveness Notes:** Naya’s dynamic is high-expectation trust: she demands peak effort from allies because she believes in them, and she does not quickly excuse avoidable underperformance.

- **Primary Role:** crafting support (crafting unlock)
- **Secondary Role (optional):** crafting support (output bonus, later progression)
- **Region:** kingdom_of_alpha
- **Location:** arkama_village
- **Sublocation:** arkama_smithy
- **Region Status:** active_now
- **Location Status:** active_now
- **Sublocation Status:** active_now
- **First Meet Context:** After the player mines ore, Lydia seeks them out and offers instruction, eager to teach anyone willing to put real effort into the craft.
- **Identity Summary:** A high-goblin smith driven by mastery and social ascent, Lydia is relentlessly productive, generous with craft knowledge, and focused on building legitimacy through excellence.
- **Questline Hook:** While high goblins are accepted, prejudice persists due to association with feral goblins; Lydia’s arc is about proving her work and discipline earn her a place in high society.
- **Personality / Social Tags:** creative, hard working, desperate, friendly, intelligent
- **Distinctiveness Notes:** Lydia is the roster’s first non-combat companion and a maximalist workhorse—she stays in constant motion, delays recruitment due to nonstop obligations, and bonds through shared labor rather than battlefield trust.

## Companion Profile: Saint Sonja

- **Companion Name:** Saint Sonja
- **Companion Type:** combat
- **Race:** human female
- **Primary Role:** support
- **Secondary Role (optional):** DPS (magic, holy)
- **Region:** kingdom_of_alpha
- **Location:** arkama_city
- **Sublocation:** arkama_sun_church
- **Region Status:** future_planned
- **Location Status:** future_planned
- **Sublocation Status:** future_planned
- **First Meet Context:** After returning from the ruined Church of Kairos Axcenda with one of her artifacts (a subspace core), the player reports the expedition to the Sun Church. The head priest reacts harshly due to church doctrine against artifact use, and Sonja intervenes to de-escalate.
- **Identity Summary:** A gentle but conflicted saint of the Sun Church, outwardly devout and diplomatic, inwardly torn between doctrine and a growing pull toward the player’s path.
- **Questline Hook:** A church prophecy was wildly misunderstood: “Only true pure love of a young maiden can unlock her true potential.” The Sun Church interprets this as Sonja needing to find love because she is the maiden, driving her questline around faith, identity, and the cost of institutional misreading.
- **Personality / Social Tags:** devout, gentle, conflicted, diplomatic, angelic, protective
- **Distinctiveness Notes:** Her core twist is a prophecy mistranslation by church leadership: she is not meant to find the prophesied person; she is that person. This creates a social arc centered on identity correction, not romantic target-hunting.
