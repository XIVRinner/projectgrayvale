## Premise
A newly formed starter-region dungeon shows impossible behavior: its core reassembles after being shattered. The player directly witnesses this and is pulled into the first investigation cycle that explains repeatable dungeon runs in-world.

## Narrative Purpose
This arc provides the lore-canon explanation for replayable dungeon gameplay while launching the hero’s long path toward RPG-scale significance.

## Conflict & Stakes
If indestructible core behavior is spreading, local containment logic fails. The risk is not one dungeon but a scalable world-level pattern that can produce repeated raids and destabilize settlement safety.

## Beats
1. Player shatters the newly formed dungeon core; it visibly reassembles.
2. Bridgitte intervenes, explains this is not unprecedented, and asks the player to keep it contained while she handles immediate response.
3. She shares prior adventurer-network reports from Omega Empira and points to the Gray Vale myth as a possible interpretive key.
4. Player objective chain begins: gather corroborating evidence, then run repeat cycles to track changes.
5. First measurable anomalies are confirmed: layout shifts and resonance/loot variance between resets.
6. Natural outbreak event begins: **Core Expansion Tide**; dungeon inhabitants raid the starter village.
7. Defense battle ends in a clean victory, but no formal status elevation for the player.
8. Chapter endpoint: global scope confirmed; Gray Vale link suggested; root cause still unknown.

## Player Outcomes
- **Immediate:** Repeatable run behavior becomes canonically grounded.
- **Mid-term:** Player becomes trusted field actor for dungeon-pattern verification.
- **Long-term:** Sets the trajectory toward wider world involvement and myth-scale investigations.

## Known Facts
- Core reassembly occurred in front of the player.
- Bridgitte confirms historical precedent via former adventurer network.
- Omega Empira reports include similar behavior.
- First outbreak pattern is natural expansion behavior, not a known sabotage event.
- Core Expansion Tide raid was repelled in the starter village.
- Phenomenon is global in scope.

## Rumors
- Gray Vale may be directly responsible rather than only correlated.
- Some dungeons may be "collecting" resources intentionally.

## Open Questions
- What is the root cause of core reassembly?
- Is expansion behavior coordinated or emergent?
- What exact mechanism links Gray Vale myth to core behavior?
