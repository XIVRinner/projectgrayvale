## Terms

### Night Elf
- **Type:** race
- **Plain Meaning:** A distinct elven people with their own social hierarchy, culture, and political duties.
- **Why It Matters:** Multiple character arcs and faction tensions depend on how Night Elf status, duty, and public expectations work.
- **First Seen:** [Kingdom of Alpha Companions](../companions/regions/kingdom_of_alpha/companions.md)
- **Related Terms:** Duchess, House Asin

### Indestructible Dungeon Arc
- **Type:** event
- **Plain Meaning:** The opening hybrid main/unlock arc where a shattered dungeon core reassembles, proving repeatable dungeon behavior is canon.
- **Why It Matters:** It explains replay loops in-world and begins the hero’s wider myth trajectory.
- **First Seen:** [Indestructible Dungeon Arc](./main-story/indestructible-dungeon-arc.md)
- **Related Terms:** Core Expansion Tide, Gray Vale

### Core Expansion Tide
- **Type:** event
- **Plain Meaning:** A natural expansion phase where dungeon influence pushes outward and inhabitants begin raids.
- **Why It Matters:** Converts dungeon anomalies from isolated curiosity into settlement-level threat.
- **First Seen:** [Indestructible Dungeon Arc](./main-story/indestructible-dungeon-arc.md)
- **Related Terms:** Indestructible Dungeon Arc

### Omega Empira
- **Type:** place
- **Plain Meaning:** A region/source context referenced in prior adventurer reports of similar indestructible-core behavior.
- **Why It Matters:** Supports the claim that starter-region events are part of a larger pattern.
- **First Seen:** [Indestructible Dungeon Arc](./main-story/indestructible-dungeon-arc.md)
- **Related Terms:** Bridgitte, Core Expansion Tide

### Gray Vale
- **Type:** other
- **Plain Meaning:** A potent myth framework believed to help interpret indestructible-core phenomena.
- **Why It Matters:** Serves as early connective tissue between local incidents and world-scale narrative meaning.
- **First Seen:** [Indestructible Dungeon Arc](./main-story/indestructible-dungeon-arc.md)
- **Related Terms:** Indestructible Dungeon Arc, Omega Empira
